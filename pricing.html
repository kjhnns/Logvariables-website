FAQ

Is there a credit card required for free?

No. Sign up with email. No card. No trial expiration.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Always. API access on both tiers. CSV export available. Your data is yours.

What happens if I stop paying?

You keep your data. You drop to free tier limits. Nothing is deleted. Ever.

Will prices change?

The free tier stays free. If the paid tier price changes, existing users keep their current price.

Is there an enterprise plan?

No. This is a personal data tool. If you need enterprise, you probably need a different product.
